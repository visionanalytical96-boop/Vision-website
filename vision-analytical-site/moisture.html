Product Overview

Halogen and infrared moisture analyzers for rapid loss-on-drying (LOD) measurement. A fast, thermogravimetric alternative for in-process moisture control of powders, granules and raw materials.

Key Features

  • Rapid halogen / IR heating
  • Loss-on-drying (LOD) determination
  • 0.01 % moisture resolution
  • Preset drying programs
  • Compact benchtop footprint
